Our rooms and the suite

Tranquillity, wood and mountain air

In our rooms, you breathe the tranquility of the Valais mountains. The larch wood, carefully crafted, surrounds you with a warmth that doesn’t need describing – you feel it.

Double Room

Double Room

Our classic double rooms offer space for two without feeling cramped – the bed is 2 meters long, perfect even for tall guests. The balcony provides enough room for two sun loungers. The larch wood on the walls and in the furniture comes from the region.

Deluxe Double

Deluxe Double Room

Our Deluxe double rooms are slightly more spacious. The balcony is more generous, and the larch wood surfaces are particularly finely crafted.

Book now
Suite

Suite

A separate bedroom with a solid double bed and rustic wooden ceiling. Open living and dining area with a white leather sofa, dining table for up to 6 people. Balcony with mountain views.

Book now
Deluxe Single

Deluxe Single Room

Our Deluxe single room with a Queen-size bed (2m x 1.60m). The wood is particularly light and welcoming.

Single Room

Single Room

Our classic single rooms – compact but with everything you need. The wood is larch, the most important native conifer of Valais.
