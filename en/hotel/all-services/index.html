Hotel

Services & Amenities

Whatever you need, we are here for you. Here you will find everything the Alpenblick has to offer, from A to Z.

A

Adapters

Power adapters and charging cables for various devices are available at the reception.

B

Baby cot

On request, we will place a cot (120/60 cm) in your room free of charge.

Bathroom stool

Available on request at the reception.

Blankets

Woollen blankets are in the wardrobe – so you can enjoy the balcony even on cooler evenings.

Books

«Die Geschichte der Augstbordwasserleitung» and «Zeneggen, Sonnenterrasse im Vispertal» by Erwin Jossen are available in the restaurant and may be purchased at the reception.

C

Catherine Lounge

The «Salon Catherine» on the first floor invites you to read, write, play or chat. Books, coffee-table volumes, magazines and board games (including Jass cards) are at your disposal.

Check-in

Your room is available from 1:30 PM at the latest.

Check-out

We kindly ask that you vacate your room by 11:00 AM on the day of departure.

Copying service

Please enquire at the reception.

Credit cards

We accept debit cards, PostFinance, VISA and MasterCard. Cash payments are also welcome.

D

Daily newspaper

The Walliser Bote is available in the restaurant.

Dinner

The menu is served from 7:00 PM. Each evening, Christoph prepares a varied menu.

Doctor

144 is the number for serious emergencies. The reception will gladly contact the local out-of-hours medical service for you.

Dogs

Well-behaved and clean four-legged friends are welcome in your room. Access to the breakfast room is not permitted. Fee: CHF 10 per day.

E

Electric vehicle charging

A charging station is available in the car park. Use the badge from reception for a discounted rate.

Emergency

In any emergency: dial 91.

Entrance door

The entrance door is locked after 10:00 PM. Your room badge allows you to enter and exit at any time.

F

Fire

A: Alert via room telephone (91) or the alarm button in the corridor. B: Close doors and windows. C: Emergency plan posted at your door; assembly point at the car park.

H

Hair dryer

The hair dryer is in the wardrobe, on top of the safe.

High chair

We have a high chair for the little ones.

Hygiene articles

Toothpaste, toothbrush, disposable razor, shaving foam, sunscreen, hand cream and other toiletries are available at the reception.

Hygiene covers

All mattresses and pillows are fitted with liquid-proof and dust-mite-proof covers.

I

Internet / Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is available free of charge. The password is available at the reception. A PC with internet access is also available in the Catherine Lounge.

Ironing service

Your laundry will be ironed and returned within the hour. An iron and ironing board are also available for self-use.

K

Key / Badge

Your room badge also opens the front door and can be left at the reception.

L

Larch wood

Josef Kenzelmann built the Alpenblick using larch wood from Zeneggen. Some rooms and the staircase still feature the original walls from 1936.

Laundry service

On request, we change your bed linen and towels daily.

Lost property

If you would like forgotten items sent on, please let us know.

Luggage

If you arrive before 1:00 PM or depart after 11:00 AM, you may leave your luggage safely at the reception.

Lunch

At midday you may order à la carte – main dishes as well as lighter and more substantial snacks.

M

Magusii

The small village shop in Zeneggen is open Monday to Saturday mornings (8–11 AM) and on Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Saturday afternoons. Please order bread rolls for your picnic the day before.

Massage

Mrs Edith Imstepf offers massages at her practice in the village. Further information in the blue folder or at www.guetlaga.ch.

N

Newspapers

Daily newspapers are available in the restaurant.

Night bell

Should you find yourself locked out, please ring the night bell to the right of the hotel entrance.

Non-smoking

All rooms are non-smoking. Smoking is permitted on the balcony – an ashtray is provided.

O

Organisation

The blue folder contains information, hiking suggestions and excursion tips. A red folder is available in the restaurant. We are happy to help you plan your holiday.

P

Parking

Free parking is available next to the barn, on both sides of the road towards Alt-Zeneggen.

Personal scales

On request, we will place a set of scales in your room.

Phone calls

Calls for you are put through to your room.

Piano

A piano is available in the Catherine Lounge.

Pillow

On request, we can provide an additional pillow, a flatter one or a neck-support pillow.

Post

A basket for outgoing post is located to the right of the reception.

R

Raclette evening

Tuesday is raclette evening: vegetable soup, raclette and fruit salad. Please let us know if you would prefer something else.

Radio

If there is no radio in your room, you will find international and national radio stations in the TV channel list.

Reception

Open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Ring the brass bell if no one is present.

S

Breakfast

From 7:30 to 10:00 AM, a generous breakfast buffet awaits you in the dining room. Coffee and tea are served at your table.

Safe

A lockable safe is in the wardrobe. Valuables may also be deposited in the hotel safe at the reception.

Shoe-cleaning kit

A shoe-cleaning kit is in the wardrobe.

Solar energy

16 m² of solar panels on the roof – the Valais sun heats our hot water.

T

Technical assistance

If you have any trouble with the television, radio, mobile phone or internet, the reception will be happy to help.

Telephone

Each room has a direct-dial telephone. Please inform reception if you make calls.

U

Umbrella

On rainy days, we are happy to lend you an umbrella.

W

Walking & Hiking

Walking maps and tips are available in the blue folder or at the reception.

Wi-Fi

Free throughout the hotel. Password at the reception.
