Biking and mountain bike in Upper Valais

Discover the Moosalp on two wheels

Zeneggen is an excellent starting point for bike holidays in Valais — quiet mountain roads, forest trails, and singletracks are quickly accessible.

From Hotel Alpenblick, the routes lead directly towards Törbel, Moosalp, and Bürchen, while Visp, as the valley station for further tours, is just a short distance away. The combination of altitude, panorama, and direct access makes the region particularly versatile.

If you’re looking for a hotel for bikers in Valais, Zeneggen combines a peaceful location with direct access to the bike areas in Upper Valais. Book directly — Location & Getting here

Season & conditions

The main season for biking in Zeneggen and on the Moosalp is during the snow-free period from late spring to autumn. After rain, natural trails are often slippery, and considerate riding is important on shared paths. Before longer tours, it’s worth checking the weather and route network.

🚲 Bike rental & infrastructure

No bike of your own? Ken’s Bikeshop in Visp offers rental MTBs and E-bikes. E-bike charging is available at the hotel. Helmets, gloves, repair kits, and enough water are essential even on shorter rides.
