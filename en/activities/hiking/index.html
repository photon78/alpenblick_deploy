Hiking around Zeneggen

The Moosalp Region — a paradise for hikers and nature lovers

Zeneggen is situated on a sunny terrace above the Vispertal and serves as a peaceful starting point for hiking holidays in Valais.

Directly from Hotel Alpenblick, you can access trails in the Moosalp region with unobstructed views of the Mischabel group, the Bietschhorn, and the side valleys of the Upper Valais. The location above Visp is convenient for day trips, while the hiking area begins right at your doorstep.

Those looking for a hotel for hiking in the Upper Valais will find short access routes to panoramic trails, high-altitude paths, and alpine summit tours here. Book directly — Location & Getting here

Season & conditions

The best time for hiking around Zeneggen and the Moosalp is from June to October. In summer, the high-altitude areas around 2000 m are ideal for longer tours, while in autumn, the golden larch trees and clear long-distance views provide particularly good conditions. On higher routes, residual snow, thunderstorms, or short-term closures may affect planning.

Practical tips

Sturdy hiking boots, rain protection, sun protection, and sufficient water should always be in your pack. The Moosalp is accessible by post bus from Visp, and individual tours can be combined with public transport or mountain railways. For planning, Swisstopo and the signposted regional routes are recommended.
