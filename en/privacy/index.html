1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

We operate Hotel Alpenblick and the website www.alpenblick.swiss and are, unless otherwise stated in this privacy policy, responsible for the data processing described herein.

This privacy policy is reviewed and updated from time to time. We recommend that you consult it regularly.

2. Contact for Data Protection

If you have questions about data protection or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us by email.

3. Collection and Processing of Personal Data

3.1 Contact

When you contact us by email, phone, or contact form, we process the data you provide (name, email address, phone number, request) as well as the time of receipt. We use this data exclusively to handle your inquiry.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3.2 Bookings

For bookings made via our website, we use a software application from Seekda and ibelsa. Your booking data (name, address, email, payment method, stay details) is stored in their systems.

More information: Seekda Privacy Policy

For bookings via third-party platforms (Booking.com, Expedia, etc.), their respective privacy policies also apply.

3.3 Booking Platforms

If you make bookings via third-party platforms (Booking.com, Expedia, Tripadvisor, etc.), we receive the necessary data for processing from the respective platform.

3.4 Review Widget

On our review page, we use a widget from Trustmary. Data may be transmitted to Trustmary in this process. More information: Trustmary Privacy Policy

4. Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics — a privacy-friendly analytics service that operates without cookies, collects no personal data, and creates no user profiles. Only anonymised, aggregated access data is recorded (e.g. pages visited, country of origin). Identification of individual persons is not possible. Use for advertising purposes is excluded.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use its own tracking cookies. Embedded third-party systems (booking widgets, review widgets, map providers) may set technically necessary cookies. We have no control over this.

6. External Services

  • OpenStreetMap — Map on the location page (openstreetmap.org/copyright)
  • Seekda — Booking widget
  • ibelsa — Booking integration
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — Privacy-compliant web analytics without cookies (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • Trustmary — Review widget

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability. Please contact us by email for this purpose.

8. Data Security

Our website is delivered via HTTPS. We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.
