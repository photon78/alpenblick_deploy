Alpine panorama

Our house overlooks Visp from 9 kilometers away—with a view of the Four-Thousanders, the sun on your face, and the serenity you only find here.

Kenzelmann Family since 1936

Three generations, one goal: for you to feel at home. No frills, but with heart.

A cuisine rooted in tradition

Light dishes, great Valaisan classics – all with vegetables from our garden and local products.

Our own wine

Our vineyard, our cellar, our pride. Come taste what grows here—straight from the barrel or the bottle.

Holidays in the heart of Upper Valais

Here, one breathes differently. The air is pure, the panorama vast, and the Alpenblick Hotel has been, since 1936, a place where guests don't just spend the night, but where they truly arrive. Our rooms are in light larch wood, with a balcony and mountain views—nothing more is needed to feel good.

In the morning, coffee from a neighboring roaster and a generous breakfast buffet. Depending on the mood, one browses through the library, plays a few notes on the piano, or settles into the living room to watch the clouds drift by. We are not a palace, but a house with character—just like Valais.
