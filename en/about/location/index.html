Location and directions

Zeneggen — on a mountain terrace at the entrance to the Visp Valley

Zeneggen lies on a sunny plateau high at the entrance to the Visp Valley.

After one of the final bends of the mountain road, a breathtaking view unfolds of the majestic Mischabel range between the Saas and Matter valleys. Opposite Zeneggen, on the other side of the valley, lies Visperterminen with the highest vineyard in Europe. To the east, the gaze sweeps beyond Naters to the music village of Ernen. The panorama is completed by the Bietschhorn towering above the Baltschiedertal to the north and the Wiwanni in the foreground.

The numerous walking trails around Zeneggen cater to all levels. A leisurely stroll through sun-dappled woodlands and fragrant dry meadows — or perhaps a trek through the alpine pastures to the Moosalp and the Augstbordhorn? Pure Valais.

1 hour to the Matterhorn
1 hour to the Aletsch Glacier
1,373 m a.s.l. Zeneggen, Upper Valais
15–20 min. from Visp

🚌 Public Transport

There is a PostBus service from Visp to Zeneggen (route B527/B528). Alight at the stop Zeneggen — Kirche, located 50 metres below the hotel.

SBB Online Timetable ↗

By Car

From Visp, take the road towards Zeneggen / Bürchen. After approximately 4 km, turn left towards Zeneggen. The journey takes between 15 and 20 minutes.

Should you wish to use the car train: tickets are cheaper when purchased online.
